Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

The Short Version

Flip Unit does not collect, store, or transmit any of your personal data. All conversions happen locally on your device. There are no analytics, no tracking, no cookies, and no third-party SDKs.

That's it. That's our privacy policy in a nutshell. Read on for the details.

No Data Collection

We believe your privacy is a fundamental right, not a feature. Flip Unit is designed from the ground up with privacy at its core:

  • No personal data collected - We don't collect your name, email, location, or any other personal information
  • No analytics or tracking - We don't track how you use the app, what you convert, or when you use it
  • No cookies - The app doesn't use cookies or similar tracking technologies
  • No third-party SDKs - We don't include any advertising or tracking SDKs from third parties
  • All conversions are local - Every calculation happens on your device and stays on your device

Currency Exchange Rates

The only network connection Flip Unit makes is to fetch currency exchange rates:

  • Rates are fetched from ExchangeRate-API, a public currency API
  • No user data is sent with these requests - only a request for the latest rates
  • Rates are cached locally on your device for offline use
  • No personally identifiable information is transmitted
  • You can use all other conversion categories without any internet connection

No Account Required

Flip Unit works instantly without any registration:

  • No login or signup required
  • No email addresses collected
  • No usernames or passwords stored
  • No social media integration
  • Just download and start converting

Local Storage Only

All app data is stored locally on your device and never leaves it:

  • Conversion history - Stored locally using SwiftData (Apple's on-device database)
  • Theme preferences - Stored in UserDefaults (iOS local storage)
  • History limit settings - Stored locally
  • Favorite categories - Stored locally
  • No cloud sync - Your data is not backed up to any external servers
  • No iCloud sync - We don't use iCloud to sync your data

No Ads or Third-Party Services

Flip Unit is a pure, standalone app:

  • No advertising networks
  • No social media integrations
  • No crash reporting services that collect user data
  • No analytics platforms
  • No user behavior tracking of any kind

App Store Review Requests

Flip Unit may occasionally ask you to rate the app using Apple's native StoreKit review prompt. Here's how it works:

  • We use Apple's built-in review request system
  • Review timing is tracked locally on your device only
  • Local tracking includes: number of conversions performed, categories used, and first launch date
  • None of this data is ever sent to us - it's used solely to determine when to show the review prompt
  • You can decline or ignore the prompt with no consequences

Children's Privacy

Flip Unit is safe for users of all ages:

  • No data collection means automatic COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) compliance
  • No age restrictions required
  • No inappropriate content
  • Safe for educational use

Data Retention

Since we don't collect any data, there's nothing to retain:

  • We have no servers storing your data
  • We have no databases with your information
  • You can clear your local conversion history at any time through the app's Settings
  • Uninstalling the app removes all local data from your device

Your Rights

Your right to privacy is fully respected by design:

  • Right to access - There's no data to access because we don't collect any
  • Right to deletion - There's no data to delete on our end; uninstall the app to remove local data
  • Right to portability - Your local data stays on your device
  • Right to object - There's no processing to object to
  • No need to contact us for data removal - we simply don't have your data

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time:

  • Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date
  • Significant changes may be announced in app update notes
  • Continued use of the app after changes implies acceptance of the updated policy
  • We encourage you to review this page periodically

Legal Compliance

Our zero data collection approach ensures compliance with major privacy regulations:

  • GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation) - EU
  • CCPA (California Consumer Privacy Act) - California, USA
  • COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) - USA
  • PIPEDA (Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act) - Canada
  • And other international privacy laws

By not collecting data, we exceed the requirements of these regulations.

We built Flip Unit because we believe utility apps should be simple, accessible, and respect your privacy. Thank you for trusting us with your unit conversions.
