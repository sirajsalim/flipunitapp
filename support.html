Getting Started

How to Use Flip Unit

  1. Choose a category

    Select from Distance, Weight, Area, Temperature, Currency, or Cooking on the home screen.

  2. Enter a value

    Type the number you want to convert using the numeric keypad.

  3. Select your units

    Tap the "From" and "To" dropdowns to choose your source and target units.

  4. See instant results

    Your conversion appears instantly at the top of the screen as you type.

The Six Categories

Distance

Convert between metric (meters, km) and imperial (feet, miles) measurements.

Weight

Convert grams, kilograms, pounds, ounces, stone, and more.

Area

Square meters, acres, hectares, square feet, and other area measurements.

Temperature

Celsius, Fahrenheit, and Kelvin conversions.

Currency

162+ world currencies with daily updated exchange rates.

Cooking

Volumes, ingredient-specific cups, and cross-category conversions.

Tips & Tricks

  • Add favorites: Swipe left on any category card and tap the star to add it to your favorites for quick access.
  • View history: Tap the History tab to see your recent conversions.
  • Change theme: Go to Settings > Theme to switch between System, Light, or Dark mode.
  • Search units: Use the search bar on the home screen to quickly find categories and units.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I convert units?

Select a category from the home screen, enter a number, choose your "From" and "To" units using the dropdowns, and see your result instantly at the top of the screen.

How do currency conversions work?

Currency rates are updated daily when you have an internet connection. The app automatically detects your locale to set default currencies. Once rates are downloaded, they're cached so you can convert currencies offline using the last known rates.

Why does my cooking conversion say "(approx)"?

Cross-category conversions between volumes (cups, tablespoons) and weights (grams, ounces) use density estimates because different ingredients have different weights. For the most accurate results, use ingredient-specific cups like "Cup of Flour" or "Cup of Sugar" which have precise density values.

How do I clear my conversion history?

Go to Settings > History Limit and select a lower number to automatically trim older entries. To clear all app data, go to iOS Settings > Flip Unit > Clear Data, or simply uninstall and reinstall the app.

How do I change the app theme?

Open the Settings tab and tap Theme. Choose from System (follows your device setting), Light, or Dark mode.

How do I add categories to favorites?

On the home screen, swipe left on any category card to reveal the favorite option. Tap the star icon to add it to your Favorites section at the top of the home screen.

Does the app work offline?

Yes! All conversions work completely offline. Currency conversions use cached exchange rates from your last online update. All other categories (Distance, Weight, Area, Temperature, Cooking) work offline without any limitations.

How many currencies are supported?

Flip Unit supports 162+ world currencies with exchange rates provided by ExchangeRate-API. Rates are updated daily when you're connected to the internet.

Is the app accessible?

Absolutely! Flip Unit is built accessibility-first with full VoiceOver support, Dynamic Type scaling, haptic feedback, Reduce Motion support, and high contrast colors. See the Accessibility section below for more details.

Why are some results in scientific notation?

Very large numbers (over 1 million) or very small numbers (under 0.001) are displayed in scientific notation for better readability. For example, 1,500,000 may display as 1.5e6.

Does Flip Unit collect my data?

No. Flip Unit collects absolutely no personal data. All conversions happen locally on your device. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

Is there an Android version?

Flip Unit is currently available only for iOS (iPhone). There are no plans for an Android version at this time.

Accessibility Support

Flip Unit is designed accessibility-first. Here's how we support users with different needs:

VoiceOver Users

  • All buttons, controls, and interactive elements have descriptive labels
  • Conversion results are announced automatically when they change
  • Unit pickers are organized with clear section headers (Metric/Imperial)
  • Full swipe gesture support throughout the app
  • Helpful hints provided for complex actions

Dynamic Type

  • All text scales from the smallest to accessibility sizes
  • Layouts automatically adapt to accommodate larger text
  • No content is cut off at any text size
  • Respects your iOS text size preference

Reduce Motion

  • All animations respect the Reduce Motion setting
  • No required motion to use any app feature
  • Smooth experience for motion-sensitive users

Visual Accessibility

  • High contrast colors that meet WCAG guidelines
  • Semantic colors that adapt to Light and Dark mode
  • Minimum 44pt touch targets (exceeding Apple's guidelines)
  • Clear visual hierarchy and focus states

Multi-Sensory Feedback

  • Visual feedback for all interactions
  • Haptic feedback for conversions and actions
  • Audio announcements via VoiceOver
